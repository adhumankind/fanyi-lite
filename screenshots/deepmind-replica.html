Our vision

We live in an exciting time when AI research and technology are delivering extraordinary advances.

In the coming years, AI — and ultimately artificial general intelligence (AGI) — has the potential to drive one of the greatest transformations in history.

  • We are a team of scientists, engineers, ethicists and more, working to build the next generation of AI systems safely and responsibly.
  • By solving some of the hardest scientific and engineering challenges, we're working to create breakthrough technologies.
